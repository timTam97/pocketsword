SimpleScripture

SimpleScripture is an independently maintained continuation of PocketSword for iPhone and iPad. It is a modified version of that project, with a Swift and SwiftUI implementation and a new visual identity. It is not an official release of PocketSword or the CrossWire Bible Society.
